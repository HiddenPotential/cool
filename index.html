How to play
  • Tap the playfield to drop a coin right there, or press DROP (or Space) to drop from the moving chute. Hold to keep dropping.
  • The shelf slides back and forth. The back wall scrapes coins off the shelf onto the table, and the shelf pushes them forward.
  • Coins pushed off the front edge are yours. Coins that fall off the sides go to the house.
  • Drop a coin through the moving LUCKY gate on the back wall to earn a slot spin. Coins take a moment to fall, so aim where the gate is going, not where it is. Match 3 for coin showers, a 10 big coin, a ★ bonus token or the 💎 jackpot gem.
  • Push a ★ off the front for 3 free spins. Push a 💎 off for 50 credits.
  • Running low? You get a free credit every 30 seconds while you have fewer than 3.
