How to play
  • Tap the playfield to drop a coin right there, or press DROP (or Space) to drop from the moving chute. Hold to keep dropping.
  • The shelf slides back and forth. The back wall scrapes coins off the shelf onto the table, and the shelf pushes them forward.
  • The front of the table is a ramp that slopes up to the edge, so loose coins slide back, and a raised lip at the very edge kicks them back again. Coins pushed over the front edge are yours. Coins that fall off the sides go to the house.
  • Coins roll down inside the back wall and slide out of the slot onto the shelf. If one rolls past the moving LUCKY window on the way down, you earn a slot spin. Match 3 for coin showers, a 10 big coin, a ★ bonus token or the 💎 jackpot gem.
  • Push a ★ off the front for 3 free spins. Push a 💎 off for 50 credits.
  • Visit the Coin Machine once a day for free coins. Come back on consecutive days to build a streak for a bigger payout.
  • Spend credits at the Claw Machine too: 5 credits a play, prizes go on your shelf.
  • Running low? You also get a free credit every 15 seconds while you have fewer than 5.

How to play
  • Spend 5 credits for a play. Steer the claw left, right, back and forward with the pad (or drag on the machine, or the arrow keys) and press GRAB (or Space) before the 20 seconds run out. Its shadow shows where it will land.
